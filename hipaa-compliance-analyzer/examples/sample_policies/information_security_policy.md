# Information Security Policy

**Policy Number:** IS-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the framework for protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic protected health information (ePHI) maintained by the organization.

## 2. Security Officer

The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) is designated as the HIPAA Security Officer, responsible for the development, implementation, and maintenance of all security policies and procedures.

## 3. Risk Analysis and Management

The Security Officer shall conduct a comprehensive risk analysis at least annually, and whenever significant changes occur to systems that store or transmit ePHI. The risk analysis shall identify threats and vulnerabilities to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of ePHI, and assess the likelihood and impact of each. Identified risks shall be remediated according to a documented risk management plan, with remediation prioritized by risk level and tracked to completion.

## 4. Access Control

Access to systems containing ePHI is granted on a role-based, minimum-necessary basis. Each user shall be assigned a unique user ID; shared accounts are prohibited. Access requests must be approved by the employee's manager and the system owner before provisioning.

Workstations shall automatically lock after **15 minutes** of inactivity.

## 5. Passwords and Authentication

Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed upon any suspicion of compromise. Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access to systems containing ePHI.

## 6. Encryption

All ePHI shall be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Portable devices (laptops, USB drives) that store ePHI must use full-disk encryption.

## 7. Audit Logging

All systems containing ePHI shall record user access and activity in audit logs. The Security Operations team reviews audit logs, access reports, and security alerts on a weekly basis and documents the review.

## 8. Malicious Software

All workstations and servers shall run endpoint protection software with automatic updates. Suspected malware infections must be reported to the IT Service Desk immediately.
