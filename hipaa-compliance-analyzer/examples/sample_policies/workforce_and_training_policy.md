# Workforce Security and Training Policy

**Policy Number:** HR-014
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs workforce access to protected health information (PHI) and required privacy and security training.

## 2. Hiring and Clearance

All candidates for positions with access to PHI shall undergo a background check prior to hire. Department managers determine the appropriate level of PHI access for each role.

## 3. Termination of Access

Upon termination of employment or when a role change removes the need for access, all system access shall be revoked **by the end of the following business day**. Badges, keys, and organization-owned devices must be returned before the final paycheck is released.

## 4. Training

All new workforce members shall complete HIPAA privacy and security training within 30 days of hire and before being granted access to PHI. All workforce members shall complete refresher training annually. Training completion is documented in the learning management system and records are retained for six years.

Periodic security reminders are distributed by email at least quarterly.

## 5. Sanctions

Workforce members who violate privacy or security policies are subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination, per the progressive discipline process in the Employee Handbook. All sanctions are documented by Human Resources.

## 6. Workstation Use

Workforce members may access PHI only from organization-managed devices. Workstations in public-facing areas must be positioned so screens are not visible to visitors. Workstations shall automatically lock after **5 minutes** of inactivity.
