# Patient Privacy Policy

**Policy Number:** PR-002
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2025

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how the organization uses and discloses protected health information (PHI) and supports patients' privacy rights.

## 2. Privacy Officer

The Director of Compliance is designated as the Privacy Officer and is responsible for privacy policies, procedures, and for receiving privacy complaints.

## 3. Permitted Uses and Disclosures

PHI may be used and disclosed without patient authorization for treatment, payment, and health care operations. All other uses and disclosures require a valid written authorization signed by the patient or their personal representative, except as required or permitted by law (including public health reporting, abuse reporting, health oversight, and disclosures required by court order).

## 4. Minimum Necessary

When using or disclosing PHI for purposes other than treatment, workforce members shall access and share only the minimum amount of PHI necessary to accomplish the purpose. Role-based access categories are maintained by the Privacy Officer in conjunction with department leaders.

## 5. Notice of Privacy Practices

The Notice of Privacy Practices is provided to every patient at first service delivery, posted in registration areas, and published on the organization's website. Registration staff shall make a good-faith effort to obtain the patient's written acknowledgment of receipt.

## 6. Patient Right of Access

Patients may inspect and obtain copies of their medical records by submitting a request to the Health Information Management (HIM) department. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days. Electronic copies are provided when the record is readily producible in electronic form. Only a reasonable, cost-based fee may be charged.

## 7. Complaints

Patients may file privacy complaints with the Privacy Officer or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. Complaints are documented and investigated. No retaliation of any kind is permitted against a person who files a complaint.
